About Home Equity Lending

Last updated November 17, 2026

Home Equity Lending LLC runs homeeqty.com. We write plain answers about home equity, HELOCs and the other ways homeowners borrow against their homes, so people can understand the tradeoffs and the risks before they apply anywhere.

What we are

We are an independent marketing affiliate of Figure Lending LLC. When a homeowner follows our link to Figure's website and starts an application, Figure may pay us. That is how this site is funded.

What we are not

  • We are not a lender, a mortgage broker or a bank.
  • We do not take applications, make credit decisions, set terms or handle loans.
  • We are not a government program.
  • We do not give financial, tax or legal advice. Our pages are general education.

How we handle your information

This site has no loan application and no homeowner form. We never ask for your Social Security number, birth date, income, credit score or employer. If you apply, you do it on Figure's own website. Our privacy policy explains what little we collect.

How we write

Every answer page states that a HELOC is secured by your home and that you could lose your home if you cannot repay. We do not publish loan pricing or promises about approval. Each page shows when it was last updated and the public sources it draws on, such as the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, the Federal Trade Commission and the IRS.

Who we serve

Our pages are written for homeowners in all 50 states.
