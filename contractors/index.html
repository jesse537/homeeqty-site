The Problem

Many stalled deals are not a sales problem. They are a funding problem.

A homeowner wants the project but does not have tens of thousands of dollars in cash on hand, even when they have built up equity in their home.

Cash only quotes go cold

Asking a homeowner to write a large check on the spot often ends the conversation. Knowing where to learn about financing keeps it going.

Equity is an asset many never touch

A home equity line generally sits alongside the homeowner's first mortgage instead of replacing it. It is secured by their home, and that is a decision only the homeowner can make.

What is the difference between a HELOC and a home equity loan?+
A home equity loan pays out one lump sum on a set repayment schedule. A HELOC is a line of credit the homeowner can draw from as needed, up to an approved limit. Both are secured by the home. [NEEDS APPROVED CLAIM: how Figure's HELOC is structured and disbursed]

Will a HELOC affect a homeowner's existing mortgage?+
A HELOC or home equity loan generally sits behind the homeowner's existing first mortgage rather than replacing it, so the first mortgage stays in place.
How does the contractor get paid?+
Figure pays the loan proceeds to the homeowner, not to the contractor. The homeowner then pays your business under the payment schedule in your own contract. [NEEDS APPROVED CLAIM: disbursement method and timing]
