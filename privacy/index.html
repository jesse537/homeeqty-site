Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what Home Equity Lending LLC (homeeqty.com, "we") collects when you use this website and what we do with it. We are an independent marketing affiliate of Figure Lending LLC. We are not a lender, and we do not take loan applications.

What we do not collect

This site has no loan application and no homeowner form. We never ask for your Social Security number, birth date, income, credit score, employer or bank details. If you apply with Figure, you do that on Figure's own website, and Figure's privacy policy covers what you share there.

What we collect

  • Partner inquiries: if a business uses the form on our contractor page, we receive the company name, contact name, work email, trade, project volume and any message. We use it only to reply.
  • Emails you send us: if you write to info@homeeqty.com, we keep the message so we can answer it.
  • Basic server logs: our hosting provider, Netlify, records standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to run and secure the site.

Links to Figure and affiliate tracking

When you click a link to Figure, it passes through an affiliate tracking service so that Figure can tell the visit came from us. That service and Figure may use cookies or similar technology under their own privacy policies. We may be paid when you visit Figure through our links or start an application. We do not receive your application details.

Links to Figure also carry short tags that show which of our pages or channels a visit came from, such as an email, a social post, a mailer or the site that sent you here. The tags hold no personal information, and your browser keeps them only until you close the tab.

Cookies and analytics

We do not currently run analytics or advertising cookies on this site. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. If we add analytics, we will update this policy first.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share homeowner contact details with contractors or anyone else. We share information only with service providers that run this site and our email, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep partner inquiries and emails only as long as we need them to respond and for ordinary business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete information we hold about you by writing to info@homeeqty.com. If we ever send you marketing email, every message will include a way to unsubscribe.
