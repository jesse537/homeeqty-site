Can self employed homeowners get a HELOC?

Written by Home Equity Lending. Last updated November 17, 2026.

Yes, self employed homeowners can apply for a HELOC, and approval depends on each lender's review of your income, credit and home equity.

Because your income does not come on a pay stub, expect the lender to ask for more paperwork, often recent tax returns, so ask early which documents it accepts.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Are your recent tax returns filed and ready to share?
  • Could you keep up HELOC payments through a slow month or a slow year?
  • Is this money for the household or for the business, and how will you keep the two apart?

Risks in plain language

  • With a HELOC, your home is the collateral. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • HELOC payments can change and can jump when the repayment period starts.
  • A slow season does not pause HELOC payments.
  • If you use the line for the business and the business struggles, you still owe the HELOC personally.
  • How interest is treated for taxes depends on how the money is used. Ask a tax professional.

Common questions

What documents do self employed borrowers usually need?
Each lender sets its own list. Many ask for recent personal tax returns and sometimes business returns or records. Ask the lender before you apply.
Do write offs affect how much income a lender counts?
Often. Many lenders look at the income on your tax return after business expenses, which can be lower than what the business brings in. Ask how the lender calculates it.
Can I use a HELOC for my business?
Lenders set their own rules on how funds may be used, so ask before you apply. The debt is yours personally and your home is the collateral.
