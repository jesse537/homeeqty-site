Can I use home equity to build an ADU in Massachusetts?

Written by Home Equity Lending. Last updated November 18, 2026.

Yes, a home equity line or loan is one way homeowners pay to build an accessory dwelling unit. Since February 2, 2025, Massachusetts allows one ADU by right in single family zones, smaller than 900 square feet or half the size of your main home, whichever is greater.

On Cape Cod, check your septic system before you borrow. A state report says some inspectors put the total cost of an ADU with a needed septic upgrade as high as $500,000.

What the Massachusetts ADU rule says
TopicWhat the state says
When it startedFebruary 2, 2025, under the Affordable Homes Act
SizeSmaller than 900 square feet or half the size of the main home, whichever is greater
WhereSingle family zoning districts in every community under the Zoning Act, which leaves out the City of Boston
Living on siteTowns may not require the owner to live on the property
How manyThe first ADU on a property is allowed by right
Short term rentalsTowns may regulate whether an ADU is used as a short term rental
Local rulesTowns may adopt reasonable regulations, with administrative approval

Questions to ask yourself

  • Can your septic system handle another unit, or will Title 5 require an upgrade? Ask your board of health early.
  • Would you rent the ADU year round, rent it short term, or use it for family, and what does your town allow?
  • If the build runs over budget, where would the extra money come from?
  • Could you keep up with payments if the unit sits empty for a while?

Risks in plain language

  • If you borrow against your home to build, your home is the collateral. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • Construction costs can run over, and you may need more than you planned.
  • Rental income is not certain, but the loan still has to be repaid.
  • Home equity line payments can change over time.

Common questions

Do I have to live on the property to build an ADU?+
No. Under the state rule, protected use ADUs may not be subject to owner occupancy requirements.
Can I rent the ADU short term?+
It depends on your town. The state law lets communities regulate whether ADUs are used as short term rentals.
Should I use a HELOC or a home equity loan?+
It depends on whether you want to draw as bills come in or take one lump sum. See our answer comparing a HELOC and a home equity loan.
