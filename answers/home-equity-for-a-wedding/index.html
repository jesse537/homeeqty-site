Should I use home equity to pay for a wedding?

Written by Home Equity Lending. Last updated November 17, 2026.

Home equity can pay for a wedding, but a HELOC used that way is debt secured by your home for a one day event, so if you cannot repay, your home is at risk.

The interest is also generally not tax deductible, because IRS rules allow the deduction only when the money is used to buy, build or substantially improve the home that secures it.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Could saving longer or trimming the budget cover more of the cost?
  • How many years would you still be paying for the wedding?
  • Would this line limit what you can borrow later for a home repair or an emergency?

Risks in plain language

  • With a HELOC, your home is the collateral. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • HELOC payments can change and can jump when the repayment period starts.
  • Wedding costs often grow, and an open line makes it easy to draw more than planned.
  • Interest on money used for a wedding is generally not tax deductible.
  • Using equity now leaves less of it for later needs.

Common questions

Is HELOC interest deductible if I use it for a wedding?
Under IRS rules, home equity interest is generally deductible only if you itemize and the money is used to buy, build or substantially improve the home that secures it. Ask a tax professional about your situation.
Does a personal loan put my home at risk?
An unsecured personal loan does not use your home as collateral. Missing payments can hurt your credit and lead to collection.
Can I use a HELOC for any purpose?
Lenders set their own rules on how funds may be used, so ask before you apply. Whatever the use, the line is secured by your home.
