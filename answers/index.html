Answers

Plain answers on using your home equity

Last updated November 17, 2026

Each page answers one question homeowners ask, compares the options side by side, and spells out the risks. Every HELOC uses your home as collateral, so if you cannot repay, you could lose your home.

Debt consolidation

Is a HELOC a smart way to pay off credit cards?

It can be, but it turns unsecured card debt into debt secured by your home, so if you cannot repay, you could lose your home.

 Comparison

HELOC or cash out refinance: which is better for me?

A HELOC keeps your mortgage and adds a line you draw from; a cash out refinance replaces your mortgage with a bigger one. Both are secured by your home.

 Business owners

Can I use a HELOC for my business?

Generally yes, within each lender's rules. The line is secured by your home, so if the business cannot support the payments, your home is at risk.

 Debt consolidation

HELOC or personal loan to consolidate debt?

A HELOC is secured by your home; a personal loan usually is not. Consolidating with a HELOC makes the old debt secured by your home.

 Comparison

HELOC or 401k loan: which is the better way to borrow?

A HELOC puts your home at risk; a 401k loan puts your retirement savings at risk and may come due if you leave your job.
