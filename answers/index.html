Is a HELOC a smart way to pay off credit cards?

A HELOC can pay off cards, but it turns unsecured card debt into debt secured by your home.

HELOC or cash out refinance: which is better for me?

A HELOC keeps your current mortgage in place and adds a separate line of credit you draw from as needed, while a cash out refinance replaces your mortgage with a bigger one and pays you the difference in cash.

Can I use a HELOC for my business?

Generally yes: money drawn from a HELOC can be used for many purposes, including a small business, though each lender sets its own rules on how funds may be used.

HELOC or personal loan to consolidate debt?

A HELOC is secured by your home and lets you draw as needed, while a personal loan is usually unsecured and paid out in one amount.

Read the answer

HELOC or 401k loan: which is the better way to borrow?

A HELOC borrows against your home, while a 401k loan borrows from your own retirement savings and is repaid through your plan.

HELOC or home equity loan: what is the difference?

A HELOC lets you draw as needed, usually at a variable rate; a home equity loan pays one lump sum, usually at a fixed rate.

Should I keep my current mortgage and add a HELOC instead of refinancing?

Adding a HELOC keeps your mortgage as it is and adds a second loan; refinancing replaces your mortgage with a new one.

HELOC or a credit card balance transfer?

A balance transfer keeps card debt unsecured; a HELOC pays cards off but makes that debt secured by your home.

HELOC or a business line of credit?

A HELOC is personal credit backed by your home; a business line of credit is made to your business.

Should I use a HELOC for college or take out student loans?

Federal student loans have federal repayment options and leave your home out of it; a HELOC uses your home as collateral.

Is it smart to use a HELOC for medical bills?

A HELOC turns medical bills into debt secured by your home. Check the bill and ask about assistance and payment plans first.

Should I use home equity to pay for a wedding?

A HELOC for a wedding is debt secured by your home for a one day event, and the interest is generally not deductible.

HELOC or reverse mortgage after 62?

A HELOC needs payments as you borrow; a reverse mortgage is for 62 and older, needs no monthly loan payment, and its balance grows.
