Can I use a HELOC to pay for a new roof?

Written by Home Equity Lending. Last updated November 21, 2026.

Yes, a HELOC can pay for a new roof if a lender approves you, but the line is secured by the home under that roof, and it takes time to open, so it may not be ready in time for a leak that needs fixing now.

The 2025 Cost vs. Value report puts the national average cost of replacing an asphalt shingle roof at $31,871 and a metal roof at $51,865. If a storm damaged your roof, ask your homeowners insurance company whether your policy covers it before you borrow.

What a new roof costs

  • Asphalt shingle roof replacement: $31,871 national average cost. Realtors estimated it adds back 67.5 percent of that at resale.
  • Metal roof replacement: $51,865 national average cost, with 50.1 percent estimated to come back at resale.
  • These are 2025 national averages from the Cost vs. Value report. Your roof's size, pitch and materials change the price, so get written estimates.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Did a storm or other event cause the damage, and have you asked your insurer whether your policy covers it?
  • Is the roof leaking now, or can the work wait while you compare offers?
  • Do you have written estimates from licensed, insured contractors?
  • Could your budget handle the payment if your rate went up or the repayment period started?
  • Do you plan to sell soon? You are generally required to pay off a HELOC in full when you sell.

Risks in plain language

  • Your home is the collateral. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • Payments can change. Many HELOCs have a variable interest rate, so the payment can move even if you borrow nothing new.
  • Payments can jump at repayment. After the draw period ends, payments are often much higher, and some plans require a large balloon payment.
  • The lender can freeze or reduce the line, for example if your home's value falls.
  • Opening a HELOC takes time, so the money may not be ready before an urgent repair is due.
  • A new roof can add less to your home's value than it costs, so you could owe more against the home than the work added.

Common questions

How much does a new roof cost?
In the 2025 Cost vs. Value report, the national average cost was $31,871 to replace an asphalt shingle roof and $51,865 for a metal roof. Your own cost depends on the size of the roof, its pitch, the materials and your area.
Will a new roof raise my home's value?
It can add some value. In the 2025 Cost vs. Value report, Realtors estimated an asphalt shingle roof replacement adds back 67.5 percent of its national average cost at resale, and a metal roof 50.1 percent. Results in your market can differ.
Should I file an insurance claim instead?
If a storm or other sudden event damaged the roof, ask your homeowners insurance company whether your policy covers it before you borrow. Your policy sets what is covered and what you pay first.
Is HELOC interest tax deductible for a new roof?
IRS Publication 936 says interest on a home equity line is deductible as home mortgage interest only when the money buys, builds or substantially improves the home that secures it, and only if you itemize. Ask a tax professional whether your roof work counts as a substantial improvement in your situation.
Should I pay the contractor up front?
The FTC says not to pay the full amount up front, notes that some states limit how much a contractor can ask for as a down payment, and says never to make the final payment until the work is done and you are satisfied with it.
Can I cancel a HELOC after I sign?
On your main home, federal law generally gives you three business days to cancel a home equity line or loan in writing, counted from when you sign and receive the required disclosures and notices.
