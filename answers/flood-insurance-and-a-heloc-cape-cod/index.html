Will I need flood insurance for a HELOC on my Cape Cod home?

Written by Home Equity Lending. Last updated November 18, 2026.

You may. If your home is in a high risk flood zone, federal rules say a regulated lender cannot make, increase, extend or renew a loan secured by the home unless it has flood insurance, and the regulators' guidance covers home equity lines as well as first mortgages. Barnstable County's Cooperative Extension says the risk of flooding on Cape Cod is high, especially in coastal areas.

Check your flood zone before you apply. High risk zones start with the letter A or V on a FEMA flood map, and you can look up your address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center or ask your town.

Before you apply

  1. Look up your address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center, or ask your town's building department for its flood maps.
  2. If you already carry flood insurance, have the policy ready so the lender can review it.
  3. Ask the lender early what flood coverage it will need, so the cost of the insurance is part of your plan.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Do you know which flood zone your home is in today?
  • If the lender needs flood insurance, can you keep paying for it along with the line?
  • After a bad storm, would repairs leave you wanting to borrow more against the same home?

Risks in plain language

  • If you borrow against your equity, your home is the collateral. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • A home equity line can have payments that change over time.
  • Borrowing against equity leaves less for you when you sell.
  • Flood insurance is a separate cost you pay on top of the home equity line.

Common questions

Does every Cape Cod home need flood insurance for a HELOC?+
No. The federal requirement is for homes in high risk flood zones, which start with A or V on a FEMA flood map. A lender can also set its own rules on top of the federal ones, so ask.
How do I find my flood zone?+
Look up your address on FEMA's Flood Map Service Center, or ask your town. Barnstable County's Cooperative Extension points homeowners to both.
I already have a mortgage. Does a new home equity line change anything?+
It can. Under the federal guidance, making a new loan secured by a home in a high risk zone is one of the moments when flood insurance has to be in place, so the home equity lender will check.
What is Increased Cost of Compliance coverage?+
It is part of a National Flood Insurance Program policy. After a flood, if your town decides repairs will cost 50% or more of the home's market value before the damage, it can pay up to $30,000 toward raising, moving or demolishing the home to meet local rules.
