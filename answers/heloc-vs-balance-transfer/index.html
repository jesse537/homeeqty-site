HELOC or a credit card balance transfer?

Written by [AUTHOR NAME, ROLE]. Last updated November 17, 2026.

A balance transfer moves card debt to another credit card, often at a promotional rate for a set time, and your home is not involved.

A HELOC pays the cards off with a line secured by your home, which turns unsecured card debt into debt your home secures, so if you cannot repay, your home is at risk.

Balance transfer and HELOC side by side
Balance transferHELOC
CollateralNoneYour home
What happens to the card debtIt moves to another card and stays unsecuredIt is paid off and becomes secured by your home
RateOften a promotional rate for a set time, then the card's regular rateUsually variable for the life of the line

How muchLimited by the new card's credit limitSet by the lender and your home equity

Questions to ask yourself

  • Could you pay the balance off before a promotional rate ends?
  • Is the balance larger than a new card's limit would likely cover?
  • Do you have a plan to stop adding new card balances once the old ones are paid?

Risks in plain language

  • With a HELOC, your home is the collateral. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • If you use a HELOC to pay off credit cards or other unsecured debt, that debt becomes secured by your home.
  • HELOC payments can change and can jump when the repayment period starts.
  • When a promotional card rate ends, the remaining balance is charged the card's regular rate.
  • Paying cards off frees up their limits. Running them up again leaves you with both debts.

Common questions

What happens when a balance transfer promotion ends?+
Any balance left is charged the card's regular rate. Read the card terms for when the promotion ends. A payment more than 60 days late can let the card company raise the rate on the whole balance.
Does a balance transfer put my home at risk?+
No. A credit card is unsecured. Missing payments can hurt your credit and lead to collection, but your home is not collateral.
Why does it matter that card debt becomes secured?+
Card debt you cannot pay can hurt your credit. Debt secured by your home can also cost you your home if you cannot repay it.
