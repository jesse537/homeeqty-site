How fast can I get a HELOC?

Written by Home Equity Lending. Last updated November 18, 2026.

There is no single timeline: each lender sets its own steps, and how fast you get a HELOC depends mostly on how quickly it can confirm your home's value, your income and who owns the home.

On your main home, you can generally cancel a HELOC within three business days after you sign, so build that window into your plans.

Questions to ask yourself

  • When do you actually need the money, and does that date leave time to read the terms?
  • Do you have recent pay records or tax returns and your latest mortgage statement ready to share?
  • Are you comparing offers on their terms, or only on how fast they close?

Risks in plain language

  • With a HELOC, your home is the collateral. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • Rushing can mean signing before you understand the rate, the payments and the repayment period.
  • If an emergency is pushing you to borrow fast, ask the people you owe whether a payment plan could give you time to compare options.
  • HELOC payments can change and can jump when the repayment period starts.

Common questions

Can I cancel a HELOC after I sign?
On your main home, you generally have three business days after signing to cancel. The right does not apply to a vacation or second home. The CFPB and the FTC explain how it works.
What can I do to avoid delays?
Each lender sets its own document list. Having recent pay records or tax returns, your latest mortgage statement and a photo ID ready can help.
Does the lender need to visit my home?
It depends on the lender. Some use an in person appraisal and others use an automated estimate. Ask which one it uses.
