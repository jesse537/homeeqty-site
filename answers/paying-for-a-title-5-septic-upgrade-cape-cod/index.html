How do I pay for a Title 5 septic upgrade on Cape Cod?

Written by Home Equity Lending. Last updated November 18, 2026.

Most Cape homeowners weigh three ways to pay: Barnstable County's Cape Cod AquiFund betterment loans, a home equity line, or savings. If it is your principal residence, the state septic tax credit can return part of the cost.

Under state rules in effect since July 7, 2023, homes in nitrogen sensitive areas may have to upgrade to an innovative or alternative system within five years unless their town gets a watershed permit, so start with your local health department.

The state septic credit

  • For tax year 2025, the credit equals 60 percent of what you spent, counting up to $30,000 of costs.
  • You can claim up to $4,000 a year and $18,000 in total for a property, carrying any extra forward for up to five years.
  • You must own the home and live in it as your principal residence.
  • Since 2024 it also covers upgrades and sewer connections required by Title 5 or a watershed permit. Ask a tax professional about your situation.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Is your home in a nitrogen sensitive area, and has your town chosen a watershed permit or the five year upgrade path?
  • Do you plan to sell in the next few years, knowing an AquiFund balance comes due at sale?
  • Do you need money for other work on the house too, or only the septic system?

Risks in plain language

  • A home equity line uses your home as collateral. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • Home equity line payments can change over time.
  • Barnstable County says AquiFund betterment loans count as a tax and cannot be subordinated to new or existing mortgages, which can matter when you refinance or add other borrowing.
  • Do not pay a contractor before AquiFund closes, because the program does not offer reimbursements.

Common questions

Do I have to upgrade my septic system?+
It depends on where you live and what your town does. Ask your local health department whether your home is in a nitrogen sensitive area and whether your town is seeking a watershed permit.
Does AquiFund have an income limit?+
Barnstable County says there is currently no minimum income requirement, but you will be asked for your most recent proof of income.
Can I claim the septic credit on a summer house?+
No. The state credit requires that you live in the home as your principal residence.
