Can I get a HELOC on a home that is paid off?

Written by Home Equity Lending. Last updated November 17, 2026.

Generally yes. A HELOC borrows against your equity, and with no mortgage, all of your home's value is equity, so the HELOC would be the only loan secured by the home.

You still need to qualify on income and credit history, and the lender will typically let you borrow only up to a set percentage of the home's value.

Before you borrow against a paid off home

  • Decide what the money is for and how you will repay it.
  • Compare a HELOC with a home equity loan, which pays one lump sum, often with a fixed rate.
  • If you are 62 or older, also compare a reverse mortgage with a HUD approved housing counselor.

Risks in plain language

  • A paid off home becomes collateral again. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • Many HELOCs have a variable rate, payments are often much higher once the repayment period starts, and some plans require a balloon payment.
  • The lender can freeze or reduce the line in certain situations, such as a drop in your home's value.
  • If you use a HELOC to pay off credit cards or other unsecured debt, that debt becomes secured by your home.

Common questions

Can I get a HELOC if I have no mortgage?+
Generally yes, as long as you qualify. With no mortgage, the HELOC would be the only loan secured by the home.
Is it easier to get a HELOC on a paid off home?+
Having more equity can help, but you still need to qualify on income and credit history, and each lender sets its own rules.
Should I borrow against a home I own outright?+
Only with a clear plan to repay. A paid off home is a strong financial position, and borrowing against it puts the home at risk if you cannot repay.
