Should I use a HELOC for a kitchen remodel?

Written by Home Equity Lending. Last updated November 21, 2026.

You can use a HELOC for a kitchen remodel and draw money as your contractor's bills come due, but every dollar you draw is debt secured by your home, and most HELOCs have a variable rate, so your payments can change.

Kitchens are costly: the 2025 Cost vs. Value report puts the national average midrange minor kitchen remodel at $28,458 and a midrange major remodel at $82,793, and Realtors it surveyed estimated a major remodel adds back about half its cost at resale.

What kitchen remodels cost

  • Midrange minor kitchen remodel: $28,458 national average cost. Realtors estimated it adds back 112.9 percent of that at resale.
  • Midrange major kitchen remodel: $82,793 national average cost, with 50.9 percent estimated to come back at resale.
  • Upscale major kitchen remodel: $164,104 national average cost, with 35.7 percent estimated to come back at resale.
  • These are 2025 national averages from the Cost vs. Value report. Your costs and your local market can differ, so get written estimates.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Do you have written estimates, and room in your budget if the work costs more than planned?
  • Will the bills come all at once or in stages as the work moves along?
  • Could your budget handle the payment if your rate went up or the repayment period started?
  • Do you plan to sell soon? You are generally required to pay off a HELOC in full when you sell.
  • Would a smaller project, or saving for part of it, mean borrowing less against your home?

Risks in plain language

  • Your home is the collateral. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • Payments can change. Many HELOCs have a variable interest rate, so the payment can move even if you borrow nothing new.
  • Payments can jump at repayment. After the draw period ends, payments are often much higher, and some plans require a large balloon payment.
  • The lender can freeze or reduce the line, for example if your home's value falls.
  • A remodel can add less to your home's value than it costs, so you could owe more against the home than the work added.
  • Construction costs can rise once work begins, so you could need more than you planned.

Common questions

How much does a kitchen remodel cost?
In the 2025 Cost vs. Value report, the national average cost was $28,458 for a midrange minor kitchen remodel, $82,793 for a midrange major remodel and $164,104 for an upscale major remodel. Your own cost depends on your kitchen, your area and the materials you choose, so get written estimates.
Is a HELOC or a home equity loan better for a remodel?
It depends on how you will pay the bills. A HELOC lets you draw as bills come due, usually at a variable rate. A home equity loan pays one lump sum, usually at a fixed rate with the same payment each month. Both are secured by your home.
Is HELOC interest tax deductible for a kitchen remodel?
IRS Publication 936 says interest on a home equity line is deductible as home mortgage interest only when the money buys, builds or substantially improves the home that secures it, and only if you itemize. Ask a tax professional whether your remodel counts as a substantial improvement in your situation.
Should I pay the contractor up front?
The FTC says not to pay the full amount up front, notes that some states limit how much a contractor can ask for as a down payment, and says never to make the final payment until the work is done and you are satisfied with it.
What about financing the contractor offers?
The FTC says never to agree to financing through your contractor without shopping around and comparing loan terms, and never to sign a document you have not read or that has blank spaces.
Can I cancel a HELOC after I sign?
On your main home, federal law generally gives you three business days after signing to cancel a home equity line or loan in writing, counted from when you sign and receive the required disclosures and notices.
