Can I use a HELOC for my business?

Written by Home Equity Lending. Last updated November 17, 2026.

Generally yes: money drawn from a HELOC can be used for many purposes, including a small business, though each lender sets its own rules on how funds may be used.

Before you do it, remember that the line is secured by your home, so if the business cannot support the payments, your home is at risk.

Taxes

IRS Publication 936 says interest on a home equity loan or line of credit is deductible as home mortgage interest only if the money is used to buy, build or substantially improve the home that secures it. The publication covers money used for business or investment in a separate section, so ask a tax professional how your use would be treated before you borrow.

Good practice for business owners

  • Borrow for a defined use with a payback plan, such as equipment or inventory that earns revenue, not to cover ongoing losses.
  • Keep a record of every draw and what it paid for. It helps at tax time.
  • Expect the lender to verify your income. If you are self employed, ask early which documents it accepts.
  • Plan for the payment to rise when the repayment period starts.

Risks in plain language

  • Your home is the collateral. If the business struggles and you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • Closing the business does not erase the HELOC. You still owe it personally.
  • Payments can change, and the lender can freeze or reduce the line if your home's value falls.
  • If you use the line to pay off unsecured business or personal debt, that debt becomes secured by your home.

Common questions

Can I use a HELOC to start a business?
Lenders set their own rules on how funds may be used, so ask before you apply. Starting a business carries more risk than growing a profitable one, and the line is secured by your home either way.
Is HELOC interest deductible if I use the money for my business?
Not as home mortgage interest. IRS Publication 936 says interest on a home equity line is deductible as home mortgage interest only when the money buys, builds or substantially improves the home that secures it. The publication covers business use separately, so ask a tax professional.
Can self employed homeowners get a HELOC?
Self employed homeowners can apply. Approval depends on the lender's review of your income and credit, so ask early which income documents the lender accepts.
