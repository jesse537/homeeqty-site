Home Equity Readiness Checklist

What to know before you tap your home equity. Five steps, about ten minutes, and no personal information needed.

Check your equity first

Last updated November 17, 2026

STEP 1

Know your numbers

  • Estimate your home value. Look at recent sales of similar homes nearby and your county assessor's record. Assessed value and market value are often different.
  • Find your mortgage balance on your latest statement. Add any other loan secured by your home.
  • Estimate your equity: home value minus everything you owe on the home. Our Equity Check does the math in about a minute, and nothing you type is saved.
STEP 2

Get clear on the purpose

  • Write down what the money is for and the total you need.
  • Decide whether you need it all at once or a little at a time. Ask each lender how it handles draws, because products differ.
  • Make a repayment plan before you borrow, based on your regular budget.
STEP 3

If you plan to pay off debt

  • Understand the trade. A HELOC is secured by your home. Using it to pay off credit cards or other unsecured debt turns that unsecured debt into secured debt. If you cannot repay, you could lose your home.
  • Have a plan to keep card balances from building back up.
STEP 4

Compare your options

  • Compare a HELOC with a cash out refinance, a personal loan and a 401(k) loan. For each one, ask: Is my home the collateral? Does it replace my current mortgage? What are the full written terms and costs, and can they change over time? What happens if I sell the home or my plans change?
STEP 5

Before you apply

  • Have your latest mortgage statement, homeowners insurance details and property tax bill handy. The lender will tell you what else it needs.
  • Ask a tax professional how your plans affect your taxes. IRS Publication 936 covers home mortgage interest.
  • Read every disclosure the lender gives you before you sign anything.

When you are ready, you can check your options on Figure's website. Read the terms carefully and compare them before you decide.
