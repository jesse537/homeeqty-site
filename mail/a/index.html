Before you borrow against it

  • A home equity line of credit is secured by your home. If you cannot repay it, you could lose your home.
  • Using it to pay off credit cards turns unsecured debt into debt secured by your home.
  • Compare it with a cash out refinance, a personal loan or a 401(k) loan, and think about how long you will stay in your home.

Quick answers

Is this a loan application?
No. This page does simple math to give you a rough estimate. It is not a loan application. If you click through, you apply only on Figure's website, and any decision and terms come from the licensed lender.
Will checking hurt my credit?
The licensed lender says checking the rates and terms you qualify for uses a soft credit pull that will not affect your credit score. If you go on to submit an application, it pulls your full credit report, which is a hard pull and may affect your credit. More on credit checks
How much could I borrow?
The licensed lender decides, based mainly on your home's value, what you owe on it, your credit history and your income. Your credit line is often smaller than your full equity. More on borrowing limits
How long does it take?
Each lender sets its own steps. It depends mostly on how quickly the lender can confirm your home's value, your income and who owns the home. More on timing
